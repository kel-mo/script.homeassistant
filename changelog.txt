v1.5.0.1
- Fixed: the dashboard could crash while states were changing
- Fixed: camera stills stopped after half an hour with a user name and password
- Fixed: the access token was sent to image servers other than Home Assistant
- Fixed: a cancelled login or a rejected token left the dashboard reconnecting forever
- Fixed: a network blip could force a new login, second factor and all
- Fixed: a change made while connecting could be missed
- Fixed: an IPv6 address in the Home Assistant URL
- Temperatures and numbers take decimals where the entity steps in them; a thermostat holding a range asks for both ends
- Summaries follow Home Assistant: the default ones appear even if the shortcut list was never edited, and empty ones are left out
- The access token is hidden in the settings

v1.5.0
- Switching a device off now asks first - two settings decide whether it asks at all, and whether lights are spared
- Opening a door now asks first, under a setting of its own
- A device's entities stand by what can be done with them, then by kind, then by name
- The dashboard opens on your favourites
- Moving from one section to another is animated
- Fixed: stepping into a section could land halfway down it

v1.4.0
- A video playing behind the dashboard shows through it

v1.3.0
- Media players open a window of their own: cover, what is playing, seeking, volume, shuffle and repeat, browsing, inputs, sound modes and joining players
- States and option lists now read the way Home Assistant words them, in your language
- A row's value has more room, and scrolls when the row is selected
- Context menus offer only what a press would change: switching on or off, never both
- The lists keep their place when the dashboard refreshes in the background
- Fixed: playing a video while the dashboard was open made the sound drop out every few seconds
- Fixed: the dashboard could freeze for half a minute while Home Assistant was answering

v1.2.0
- The section tiles no longer count their entities, which leaves the names more room
- The energy summary is called "Energy" now: it lists what the energy dashboard is built from, it does not add up a day

v1.1.0
- Venetian blinds: open, close, stop and set the slats
- Fans: speed, preset, oscillation and direction
- Humidifiers: target humidity and mode
- Valves: open, close, stop and position
- The changelog is now shown in Kodi's addon browser
